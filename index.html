MCP Team

Martin Capital Partners, LLC, is an Oregon-based, SEC Registered Investment Advisory firm managing $306 million in client assets*. Investment Philosophy

We seek to build investment portfolios that produce consistent, absolute returns, with mitigated risk and volatility. To accomplish this, we leverage a long-term horizon, aimed at capitalizing on the power of reinvested and compounded dividends from high-quality companies with distinct competitive advantages.
